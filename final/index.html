STATS 401 · Final Project

Flight Delay Analysis: Geography, Causes, Weather, and Carriers

A coordinated visual investigation of U.S. flight disruptions from January 2020 to June 2026, built from 146,438 cleaned carrier–airport–month records merged with NOAA weather observations. The dashboard moves from geographic overview to cause decomposition, weather association, and carrier comparison.

Delay Map · Cause Composition · Weather Scatter Airline Performance Weather Association Profile
VISUALIZATIONS 1–3

Where Delays Concentrate, What Causes Them, and How Weather Relates

The U.S. airport delay map (bubble size = arrivals; color = delay rate or weather-delay share), the cause-composition breakdown for the ten busiest airports (sortable and spotlightable), and the precipitation–delay scatter plot with brushing and a fitted trend line.

VISUALIZATION 4

Airline Performance: Reported Flight Volume vs. On-Time Rate

Twenty-five carriers aggregated from all 146,438 cleaned records. Horizontal position encodes total reported flights; vertical position encodes the flight-weighted on-time rate. The embedded view provides date and airport filters, pinned carrier details, highlighting, axis toggle, reset, and CSV export.

VISUALIZATION 5

Weather Association Profile: Heatmap, Network, and Sortable Table

Mean absolute Spearman correlation between each weather factor and the selected disruption outcome, aggregated across airports over a chosen historical window. The geographic similarity network connects airports sharing the same dominant factor and correlation direction; the sortable table and monthly playback timeline support detailed exploration.
