Weather Drivers of Flight Delays Across U.S. Airports

STATS 401 Final Project · BTS On-Time Performance + NOAA weather · 224 U.S. airports, Jan 2020 – Jun 2026 · 30.8M arrivals
Flight Delay Dashboard 1 · Weather-Delay Associations 2 · Airline Performance 3 · Airport Delay Map 4 · Delay Causes 5 · Weather vs. Delay
How to read this dashboard. Weather–delay associations are strongly seasonal — monthly Spearman |ρ| frequently exceeds 0.5 for snowfall and precipitation in winter but collapses in summer — while at the biggest hubs delays are dominated by late-arriving aircraft (34.4% of delay causes) rather than local weather (national median 5.5%). Explore each view below, or open any view in a full tab.
1. Weather-Driver Profile — Impact Overview, Association Network & Airport Table Open full page ↗
Mean |Spearman ρ| heatmap by weather factor × month · geographic network linking airports with the same dominant weather factor · sortable, searchable airport table. Use the global controls (outcome, association window, airline, airport), the timeline playback, the |ρ| threshold slider, and click a heatmap cell or table row to filter/highlight the network. CSV upload fallback is available at the top of the embedded page.
2. Airline Scale & Reliability Open full page ↗
One point per carrier: reported flights (x) vs. flight-weighted on-time rate (y), with date-range and airport filters, click-to-pin details, airline highlighting, a 0–100% axis toggle, and CSV export of the current selection.
3–5. Airport Delay Map · Delay Causes · Weather vs. Delay Rate Open full page ↗
Three coordinated views: a U.S. bubble map (zoom/pan, metric toggle, tooltips), 100% stacked bars of delay-cause composition at the ten busiest hubs (sort buttons, legend spotlight), and a precipitation-vs-delay scatterplot with brush filtering, a fitted trend line, and reset. All insights are computed live from the data.
Data: data/airport_weather_monthly.csv (224 airports × 78 months) and data/flight_weather_monthly.csv (carrier-level).
